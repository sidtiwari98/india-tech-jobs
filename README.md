# Tech jobs in India, from company career pages

Open tech roles in India, taken from each company's own career page and rebuilt every morning by [ASAI](https://www.asaitech.in/?utm_source=github&utm_medium=list&utm_campaign=root). Every row shows the date the role first went up. A role that closed and came back is marked reopened, with both dates.

- [3+ years](experienced/README.md), one page per role family: 3,237 roles shown, 1,252 of them reopened
- [0 to 2 years](early-career/README.md): 465 roles shown, 164 of them reopened

The lists show the newest 5 roles per company in each role family. 2,599 more open roles are counted under the tables, by company.

Each row has two links. **Apply** goes to the employer's own posting. **Fit and details** opens the role on ASAI.

- Every role comes from the company's own career page, not from job boards or agencies.
- Generated 2026-11-27 05:48 UTC (2026-11-27 11:18 IST). Roles that close are removed at each run.
