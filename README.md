# Tech jobs in India, from company career pages

Two lists of open tech roles in India, updated daily by [ASAI](https://www.asaitech.in/?utm_source=github&utm_medium=list&utm_campaign=root).

- [3+ years](experienced/README.md), one page per role family: 6,215 roles
- [Freshers and 0 to 2 years](early-career/README.md): 516 roles

Each row has two links. **Apply** goes to the employer's own posting. **Fit and details** opens the role on ASAI.

- Every role comes from the company's own career page, not from job boards or agencies. ASAI checks these pages every hour (a few large career sites take up to 5 hours a round).
- Generated 2026-11-26 13:42 UTC (2026-11-26 19:12 IST). Roles that close are removed at each run.
